## What does it do?

You type something like:

> *"I'm stuck in Dubai and my flight to New York got cancelled. Help!"* or Maybe something like due to the airspace restriction my flight to the India got cancelled and I want to fly back home somehow, help me how can I reach home.

And the website figures out other ways to get you there.

It tells you:
- A better route 
- How much it will roughly cost
- Whether you need a Visa  for any stop along the way
- Whether any part of the sky is currently closed for flying
- if The weather is bad

It also has a little assistant you can **talk to out loud** — like Siri, but for travel.
You just say your question and it talks back to you.I have named it Aria. It is your personal AI travel Assistant.
