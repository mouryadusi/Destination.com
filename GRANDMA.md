# GRANDMA.md

Hi Grandma! 👋

You know how sometimes flights get cancelled and you have no idea what to do next?
Like when a storm closes an airport and everyone is just standing around confused?

**That's the problem I solved.**

---

## What does it do?

You type something like:

> *"I'm stuck in London and my flight to New York got cancelled. Help!"*

And the website figures out other ways to get you there.

It tells you:
- A better route (maybe go through Dublin instead)
- How much it will roughly cost
- Whether you need a special stamp in your passport (a visa) for any stop along the way
- Whether any part of the sky is currently closed for flying

It also has a little assistant you can **talk to out loud** — like Siri, but for travel.
You just say your question and it talks back to you.

---

## How does it work?

Imagine a very well-read travel agent who has read every airline timetable,
every government travel warning, and every airport guide — and can answer in half a second.

That's basically what's happening behind the scenes.
I didn't build that brain myself. I borrowed one (called Groq/Llama) and taught it
to answer specifically about flights and travel.

---

## What else is on the website?

- A little **flight game** where you fly a plane and dodge storm clouds ☁️
- A **quiz** that tests if you know what "DXB" means (it's Dubai airport!)
- A live ticker at the bottom showing real flight disruptions around the world

---

## Did I build all of this myself?

Honestly? Most of it, yes.

I wrote all the code that makes the website look the way it does —
the dark screen, the glowing cursor, the animated plane trails.

The "smart" part — answering your travel questions — uses an AI tool I connected to.
I didn't build the AI. I built the website around it, told it what to do,
and made sure it gives you useful answers instead of random ones.

It's like I built the restaurant. Someone else made the chef.

---

## Does it actually work?

Yes! You can try it at:

👉 **https://destination-com-dun.vercel.app**

Type something like "I want to fly from Dallas to Mumbai" and it will give you real options.

---

## One honest thing

The flight prices and times it gives you are **estimates** — not real bookings.
Think of it like a very smart friend giving advice, not a travel agent with a ticket machine.
Always double-check before you book anything for real.
