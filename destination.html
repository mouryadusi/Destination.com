How it works

From stuck to sorted in under 60 seconds.

Three interconnected systems work simultaneously the moment you describe your situation — no forms, no dropdowns, no rigid fields.

01 / Input
🗣

Tell us in plain language

Type your situation naturally — where you are, where you need to be, your constraints. No structured forms. Our LLM parser extracts intent, urgency, origin, destination, budget signals, and modality preferences from conversational text.

02 / Analysis
🧠

Real-time restriction mapping

We simultaneously query live airspace NOTAMs, rail timetables, ferry schedules, and geopolitical advisories. A deep learning model cross-references your route against 72-hour historical disruption patterns to flag likely delays before they hit.

03 / Optimisation
⚡

Three-axis route scoring

Each candidate route is scored on cost, total journey time, and feasibility complexity. The AI balances your stated priority (speed vs. budget) against practical thresholds — filtering out technically possible but unreasonably complex options.

04 / Community
👥

Live traveller signals

Routes are enriched with anonymised insights from travellers currently on similar journeys — real wait times at borders, actual connection reliability at hub airports, and recent firsthand condition reports that no database holds yet.

Platform capabilities

Built for the moments travel goes wrong.

🛰

Live Airspace Intelligence

Continuously ingests NOTAM feeds, bilateral airspace agreements, and geopolitical advisories. Routes are automatically re-scored when a restriction changes — no manual refresh needed.

🔀

Multi-modal Pathfinding

Flights, high-speed rail, intercity buses, and ferries are treated as equal citizens. The engine can construct journeys spanning multiple transport types when a single-mode solution is unavailable or inefficient.

📊

Three-Axis Scoring

Every route is evaluated on cost, total journey time, and feasibility complexity simultaneously. You choose which axis matters most; the AI respects that priority while ensuring the route remains practically achievable.

⚠️

Disruption Prediction

A deep learning model trained on three years of delay data identifies connection risks before they materialise — flagging routes where weather, strike, or capacity patterns suggest a problem window in the next 72 hours.

🔒

Privacy-First Community

Traveller insights are aggregated and anonymised before surfacing. No user is ever identifiable within shared data. Optional direct connection with fellow travellers requires mutual opt-in.

🌐

Conversational Interface

No dropdown menus, no rigid airport code fields. Describe your situation in natural language — including emotional context like "I need to get to my mother's bedside" — and the AI extracts the relevant constraints automatically.

Under the hood

AI that understands the physics of travel.

Not a flight search engine with an AI label. A purpose-built routing intelligence trained specifically on multi-modal disruption scenarios.

  • 01

    LLM Intent Extraction

    Parses natural language inputs to extract origin, destination, time horizon, budget signals, modality preferences, and urgency level — handling ambiguous or emotionally-charged inputs gracefully.

  • 02

    Graph-Based Route Optimisation

    Transport networks are modelled as weighted directed graphs. A modified Dijkstra algorithm with time-dependent edge weights accounts for connection windows, gate changes, and real-time delays.

  • 03

    Deep Learning Disruption Forecasting

    A transformer model trained on historical NOTAM patterns, weather correlations, and labour action cycles predicts disruption probability per route segment over a 72-hour window.

  • 04

    Community Signal Integration

    Anonymised traveller reports are NLP-classified into structured insights (wait time, seat availability, connection reliability) and injected into route scoring as real-time edge weight adjustments.
